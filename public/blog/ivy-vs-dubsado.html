Ivy vs Dubsado: Which Fits Your Solo Business?

Dubsado is beloved for deep workflow customization at $35-$55/month. Ivy trades some of that depth for breadth, AI, and $8.99/week. An honest breakdown.

2026-04-27 · 6 min read · Ivy Blog

Dubsado has one of the most devoted user bases in solo-business software, and it earned it: the form builder and workflow engine are deeply customizable, and the pricing ($35/mo Starter, $55/mo Premier, per their public pricing) hasn't chased the market upward. If you love tinkering with automation logic, Dubsado rewards you.

So why would anyone pick Ivy instead? Different philosophy.

Choose Dubsado if…

You genuinely enjoy building custom workflows, your business runs on elaborate branded forms, and you'll invest the setup weekends. Its power-user ceiling for form logic is higher than Ivy's.

Choose Ivy if…

You want the outcome (booked, reminded, signed, paid, followed-up) without becoming your own systems administrator. Ivy's bet is that a solo owner shouldn't need a setup course: describe the automation in plain English and the AI builds it, and the website, campaigns, and client portal are already there.
