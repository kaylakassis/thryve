Ivy vs Vagaro for Salons, Spas & Solo Beauty Pros

Vagaro starts around $30/month per calendar plus add-ons for texts, forms, and websites. How Ivy's flat $8.99/week compares for a one-chair business.

2026-04-20 · 6 min read · Ivy Blog

Vagaro is a heavyweight in beauty and wellness - marketplace exposure, salon-specific features, POS hardware. For multi-chair salons it's a natural shortlist item. The solo-stylist math looks different, mostly because of how the pricing is structured.

Vagaro's public pricing starts around $30/mo for one bookable calendar, with additional calendars, and add-ons like text marketing, forms, and branded websites priced separately. Card processing runs roughly 2.2%-3.5% depending on plan and method.

Choose Vagaro if…

You're a salon or studio with multiple staff, you want its consumer marketplace as a discovery channel, or you need salon-specific POS hardware. Those are real advantages Ivy doesn't claim.

Choose Ivy if…

You're a business of one and the add-on menu is the problem: by the time texts, forms, and a website are bolted on, the "cheap" plan isn't. Ivy's flat price includes the full kit, plus an AI assistant that chases quiet clients and drafts your rebooking messages - the follow-up work that actually fills a solo chair.

One price, nothing bolted on

Booking, SMS reminders, forms, website, gift cards, memberships - included in Ivy's one plan. No add-on menu.
