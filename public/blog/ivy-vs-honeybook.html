Ivy vs HoneyBook: An Honest Comparison for Solo Businesses

HoneyBook runs $36-$129/month plus 2.9% + 25 cents per card payment; Ivy is $8.99/week with no platform transaction fees. Where each one actually wins.

Where HoneyBook wins

Long-form proposals with embedded payment schedules are HoneyBook's home turf - if your sales motion is "send a beautiful multi-option proposal to a $6,000 wedding client," it's built exactly for that. It also has years of ecosystem maturity: templates, communities, and integrations.

Where Ivy wins

Breadth per dollar and payments math. Ivy folds in the website, email campaigns, memberships, and an AI assistant that acts on your data - pieces that are extra tools alongside HoneyBook. And because payments go straight to your own Stripe with no platform percentage, your fee doesn't grow with your revenue.

The one-sentence version

Proposal-driven project businesses with bigger contracts: HoneyBook earns its price. Appointment-driven businesses of one who want the whole operation in one login: that's who Ivy is for.
